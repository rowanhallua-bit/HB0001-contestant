# Bidd brand kit: guide page and templates

Scope: the Bidd identity (symbol, wordmark, type, color, voice) shipped as a code-ready kit in `brand/`, a shareable brand guide page (`brand/index.html`, Read mode: the team and judges understand and grab the brand), and social/deck templates (`brand/templates/`, Persuade mode).
Audience: Quinn (frontend: needs tokens, components, icons now), Rowan (marketing: needs PNGs and templates for Canva, the deck and Instagram), Rouan, and the hackathon judges.
Constraints: black and white only for Bidd itself, and the campus color fills the rest (Bidd frames, campus fills). References are Nike, Supreme, Depop. Google Fonts only. No Greek-org or university marks, no real partner brands, no invented quotes or claims.
Unresolved: final photography (Rowan shoots it).
Revision (2026-10-17): Quinn replaced the first-round 1/1 symbol and lowercase wordmark with the B1DD wordmark and B1 mark chosen in the logo lab (brand/logo-lab.html), and Archivo body text with Hanken Grotesk.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Bidd is a drop brand that happens to be a marketplace: Nike's type confidence and imperative voice, Supreme's one-mark scarcity, Depop's photo-first peer energy, in black and white with the campus filling the slash. It refuses pastel sorority clip-art, cream-and-serif editorial, SaaS-generic, and university-official.

OWN-WORLD: True black #0B0B0B and paper white #FFFFFF with neutral grays, and no Bidd hue. The campus color is only ever a fill: the 1 in the B1DD logo, drop bars, active states, Cup bars. Archivo Extra Condensed Black Italic in caps for headlines (tight leading) and, outlined, for the B1DD wordmark and B1 mark. Hanken Grotesk for text. Square photo crops, black pill actions, pill chips.

STORY: In one look the reader sees the mark, its campus swap, and the voice ("Be 1 of 1."). They believe Bidd is a real drop brand rather than a class project. They copy the SVG and code they need from the page, and the files live in the repo under brand/ (artifact pages cannot start downloads).

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed black. The outlined B1DD wordmark runs nearly the full width at up to about half the viewport height, its 1 in the live campus color. Under it, one row: "BE 1 OF 1." in condensed black italic caps at display max, the tagline line, and the pill. The B1DD lockup is top-left, with section links to the right. A campus switch (UA / Ole Miss / any color) and a white "Get the kit" pill (jumping to the kit section) sit along the bottom edge, and changing campus repaints every campus fill on the page at once while the hero 1 re-stamps in its new color.

FORM: The category standard, user-pinned, with the user-chosen B1DD logo: the streetwear/sportswear drop-brand canon as Nike, Supreme and Depop practice it, played straight. Quinn rejected the dealt hand (Sheet Banner, Bid Card, generic resale). Seed key c4519796.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
